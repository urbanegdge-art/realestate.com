Documentation & Title

The documentation depends on the property and its title. Generally, clients may receive documents such as a Deed of Assignment, Registered Survey, Receipt/Contract of Sale, and other relevant estate or title documents applicable to the property.

Yes. Our team guides clients through the documentation process and explains the documents applicable to their property. Where documentation fees apply, the client will be informed before payment.

Where applicable to the property, yes. A Deed of Assignment is one of the important legal documents used to transfer an interest in land from the seller/assignor to the buyer/assignee.

A Registered Survey helps establish the location, boundaries, and survey information relating to a parcel of land. It is an important document for confirming the surveyed position of the property and for further land documentation.

UrbanEdge currently has a limited promotional offer on qualifying properties where clients can receive a Deed of Assignment and Registered Survey at no additional cost, subject to the terms and duration of the promotion.

Yes. Promotional offers are time-limited. The current offer is available for 60 days, subject to the stated terms and conditions. Clients are encouraged to confirm eligibility before making a purchase.
